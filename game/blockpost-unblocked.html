Block-Style FPS With Real Depth

Blockpost is a first-person shooter built in a voxel art style that's immediately familiar to anyone who's played Minecraft, but the gameplay is pure FPS. Playing Better Than Random

  • Learn one weapon's recoil first: Every weapon has a distinct recoil pattern. Spray a wall in an empty server to see the pattern, then practice controlling it back down. Most players never do this and wonder why they miss.
  • Aim Down Sights for range: Hipfire is for close range only. Right click to ADS on any target more than 10 meters away. The accuracy difference is significant.
  • Sniper Arena to practice positioning: Sniper mode forces you to learn map angles and positioning without the pressure of constant close-range fights. The skill transfers back to other modes.
  • Team Deathmatch before FFA: Free For All is chaotic. Team Deathmatch gives you allies and a more structured engagement. Better for learning map layouts without being attacked from all angles.
  • Crouch while shooting: Crouching reduces your hitbox and improves accuracy. Experienced players crouch during most firefights. It's one of the biggest mechanical advantages available.
