Madalin Stunt Cars 2 Unblocked: 34 Supercars, No Finish Line, Full Nitro

There is no race. No checkpoint. No finish line. Madalin Stunt Cars 2 Unblocked hands you a Bugatti Veyron and three open maps filled with ramps, loops, and corkscrew jumps. What you do with that is entirely up to you. Madalin Stanciu built this in July 2015 and it became one of the most-played browser driving games ever made. Open Sandbox, All Cars Unlocked

All 34 cars are unlocked from the start. Bugatti Veyron, Lamborghini Aventador, Ferrari LaFerrari, Dodge Charger, and more. Pick one, pick a map, and drive. Three maps cover different terrain styles. Online multiplayer lets you join servers with other players doing the same thing simultaneously. Color customization changes your car's appearance before you start. No currency, no unlocks, no progression wall.

Controls

  • WASD or Arrow Keys: Accelerate, brake, steer
  • Shift: Nitro boost (blue flames from exhaust, fast refill)
  • Space: Handbrake for sharp turns and drifts
  • R: Reset car position if stuck
  • C: Change camera angle

Getting the Most Out of the Maps

  • Shift before ramps, not during: Activate nitro on the flat approach to a ramp for maximum launch height. Activating mid-ramp gives inconsistent results and wastes the boost.
  • Handbrake for drift entry: Tap Space while cornering to break rear traction and initiate a drift. Hold Space and steer to hold the drift. Release to grip and accelerate out.
  • Lighter cars for corkscrew ramps: The heavy muscle cars lose momentum on vertical loops. The lighter supercars handle sustained vertical track segments better.
  • Reset (R) saves time: If you land badly and the car is upside down or stuck, just press R. No penalty, instant reset. Use it constantly rather than trying to recover bad landings.
  • Desert map has the biggest jumps: If your goal is maximum air time and distance, the desert map has the longest ramp setups. The city map is better for drifting. The original map is the most varied.

Developer

Madalin Stunt Cars 2 was created by Madalin Stanciu, an indie developer from Romania, and released July 2015. Updated in November 2017. Stanciu built the entire MSC series solo. The top speed in-game is 431 km/h using the Bugatti Veyron with nitro. The game runs in browser using WebGL for full 3D rendering without any plugins.
