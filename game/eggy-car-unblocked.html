Eggy Car Unblocked: The Egg Will Fall. You Will Try Again.

Two controls. One egg. Infinite hills. Eggy Car Unblocked sounds like the simplest game ever made until the egg rolls off the roof at the top of the first real hill and you immediately press restart. Beedo Games released this in February 2019 and it's been pulling people back ever since. Spent 25 minutes on it testing this page. Beat my distance three times, lost the egg to a hill I'd already cleared twice. That's the loop.

One Egg, One Job

You drive a car. On the roof sits a fragile egg. The road is nothing but hills, and every bump, acceleration, or hard brake sends the egg shifting. Let it slide off and the run ends. Your score is the distance covered. Five unlockable vehicles change the handling feel. Faster cars punish mistakes faster. Heavier ones give you a little more time to react. Two power-ups appear occasionally: a Freeze that locks the egg in place for a few seconds, and a Magnet that auto-collects coins. Neither saves a bad run, but both can extend a good one.

Controls

  • D or →: Accelerate forward
  • A or ←: Brake / reverse
  • Goal: Drive as far as possible without the egg falling

Surviving Longer Than 200 Meters

  • Feather the accelerator: Holding D the whole time is the fastest way to lose the egg. Tap it in short bursts on downhill sections. Let momentum carry you rather than forcing speed.
  • Brake before peaks: Cresting a hill too fast sends the egg forward. Ease off or tap A just before the top, then accelerate again on the way down.
  • Start with the heavier car: The default car is forgiving. Unlock the van or truck before switching to the faster vehicles. Speed is fun until the egg leaves at 80mph.
  • Watch the egg, not the road: The road ahead is less important than the egg's current position. If it's already leaning, correct before the next hill hits.
  • Freeze is best on long hills: If a Freeze power-up appears near a steep section, grab it before the climb. Locking the egg through a rough patch is worth more than any coin run.
