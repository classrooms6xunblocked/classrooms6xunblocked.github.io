Grow or Get Cut

Your worm grows by eating sweets scattered across the map. The bigger you get, the higher your score and the more dangerous your trail becomes to smaller worms. Cut in front of an opponent to force them to crash into your body. They disappear, leaving food you can collect to grow faster. But you're always one bad turn away from running into someone else's body and disappearing yourself. Sessions run 3 to 5 minutes before something ends them. That's the whole game.

Power-Ups Change Everything

Speed boost lets you dash forward: useful for cutting off opponents or escaping tight situations. The magnet pulls nearby food toward you automatically. Size multipliers accelerate your growth rate. Each power-up appears randomly on the map. Prioritizing them over food can be the difference between dominating a server and being eliminated in the first minute.

Controls

  • Mouse: Steer your worm in the cursor direction
  • Left Click or Space: Speed boost (uses mass)
  • Objective: Cut in front of opponents to eliminate them

Surviving Longer Than 2 Minutes

  • Stay near the edges early: The center of the map is chaos when you're small. Hug the edges, collect food safely, and grow before engaging other players.
  • Speed boost for cutting, not escaping: Using your speed boost to run away wastes it. Use it to cut in front of an opponent while you're already alongside them.
  • Coil around smaller worms: Once you're large enough, circle a smaller worm to trap them. They'll crash into your body trying to escape.
  • Magnet power-up is highest priority: Auto-collecting food while you focus on positioning is a massive advantage. Grab the magnet over everything else.
  • Don't boost into tight spaces: Speed boosting into a cluster of large worms is the fastest way to end a good run. Only boost when you have a clear exit route.
